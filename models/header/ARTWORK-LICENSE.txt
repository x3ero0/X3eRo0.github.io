The GNU relief in gnu-linux.glb preserves the original detailed geometry from
the user's GNU/Linux coin STL. That relief was adapted from the original SVG.
These adaptations are
distributed under the same CC BY-SA 2.0 license. GNU is a trademark of the
Free Software Foundation.

gnu-linux-ps1.glb contains surface maps baked from the same relief. These
adaptations are also distributed under CC BY-SA 2.0.

The Tux portrait geometry in the source coin was traced from the user's coin
photographs. Tux is based on Larry Ewing's Linux penguin, created using the
GIMP. This reconstruction is not an assertion of ownership of the underlying
mascot or token design.

Dunlop names and graphics belong to their respective owners. The Flow Gloss
pick is an independent reconstruction of the user's photographed object.
